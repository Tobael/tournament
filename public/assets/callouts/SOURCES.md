The clips are trimmed to short cues, leading silence removed, normalized to -18
LUFS/-2 dBTP and encoded as mono 44.1 kHz MP3. They are hosted locally; there are no
soundboard embeds or third-party requests during playback. The fallback arcade
cues and TV result chime are generated by this app with Web Audio oscillators.
